Three more ways around the panel, built from your notes

All three keep what you liked in round one: the coloured pads for Sound, Shape, Motion and Effects, the slots, and the map. All three also add what you asked for. The sounds sit one level above the editing. Pad 8 is now Back. The black main knob moves something in every view, and the words beside it say what it does right now. They differ in how deep you go and what knob 5 does. Turn knobs with the mouse wheel or by dragging up and down, and click the main knob to press it. Keys: ←→ turn the main knob, ↑↓ turn knob 5, Enter presses it, Esc or 8 is Back, 0 is holding Back, 1–7 are the pads, and holding Shift is the Shift button. Round one is still here.

What each control does here

On the MiniLab 3 itself

Two short lines, about 18 and 15 characters. Line one says where you are, and line two says what the main knob is on. A turned knob shows its name and value for a second.

What I took from your notes

  • Sounds one level up. The top of everything is a sound list: turn to look, press to load, and knob 5 jumps a room. The loaded sound's four macros stay on knobs 1–4.
  • The main knob navigates in every view. It moves through sounds, blocks, slots, pages or a type list, and its job is printed beside it. It never sits idle.
  • A real Back button. Pad 8: tap it to go up one level, hold it to jump to the sounds. The Hydrasynth has EXIT and HOME buttons for this, and Elektron uses its NO key the same way. Shift + pad 8 still switches part A and B.
  • Colours and slots stay. Pads 1–4 are Sound, Shape, Motion and Effects in their own colours, and the same colours mark the map.
  • The map picks the slot. D and F show the whole map. E shows only the part of the map that belongs to the pad you pressed.
  • Where you are is always one line. A trail under the title reads, for example, Neon Brass › Shape › P1 Ladder › Filter. The ◎ and 5 marks show which part each of the two knobs moves.

The same in all three

  • Knobs 6–8 are Glide, Brightness and Space on every page.
  • Faders are the mixer: Part A, Part B, Effects and Master, with pickup.
  • Shift + any knob turns it ten times finer. Shift + press is a second Back.
  • Pads 1–4 open their section; press the lit pad again for the next slot. Shift + pad 1 opens Perform (chord, arp, voice).
  • Pads 5–7 stay free for the recorder.
  • Pad 8 is Back. Its light dims when there is nowhere left to go.
