# Macro drafts: banks 04 to 07 (Observatory, Greenhouse, Basement, Studio 1982)

Scaling used below: cutoff amount 0.1 is about one octave; pitch amount 1 is 48 semitones (0.005 is
about a quarter of a semitone); `envN.time` and `lfoN.rate` amount 0.25 is one octave (twice as long,
twice as fast). Every per-voice LFO used for Movement is one the preset does not set, so it runs at its
default (sine, 2 Hz, restarts on each note); Movement speeds it up or slows it down with a
`src=macro4 dst=lfoN.rate` line, so depth and speed grow together as the knob turns.

- Swell: slower in and longer out, up to twice as long (6 s in, 8 s out).
- Gusts: the two bands sway against each other, up to about 4 semitones, at a slow 0.5 Hz on top of the long drift.
