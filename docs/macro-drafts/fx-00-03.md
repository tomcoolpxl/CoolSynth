# Effect routes: 00-workshop, 01-bedroom, 02-arcade, 03-outrun (D179 draft)

Every line below is `src=macroN` (adds nothing at macro 0), at most two `fx` lines per preset (limit 8),
on slots the preset already sets, by the key of that slot's type (all `modulatable` in `Inserts.h`).
No preset in these four banks has a part B, so there are no `b.fx` lines. No mix is raised and no
name changes, so there are no `names:` lines.

What the amounts mean (normalised 0..1, from `Inserts.h` and the effect code):
- Reverb `size` is linear and sets the plate's decay 0.25 + 0.70 * size; the tails quoted are the
  RT60s of D178 (2.4 s at 0.2, 4.4 s at 0.5, 10.5 s at 0.8, damping 0.3), interpolated with the
  same law (about 2.2 / -ln(decay)); at these presets' damping 0.45 to 0.6 they are a little shorter.
- Delay `feedback` is linear over 0..0.85, so +0.2 normalised is +0.17 in feedback. The highest at full
  is 0.52 (Laser Lead), far under 0.9; the range itself stops at 0.85.
- Chorus `depth` is linear, 0..2.2 ms of sweep around the centre delay.
- LoFi `wow` is linear up to about 14 cents of slow 0.45 Hz drift; `hiss` up to -30 dBFS, following
  the signal; `bandwidth` closes a 2-pole low-pass as 1.5 kHz * 13.3^bandwidth.

Level with all four macros up: the only rises are a longer reverb or delay tail (a bigger plate
holds more energy in a sustained chord: about +1.5 to +2.5 dB on the wet signal, which is a quarter
or less of the mix, so well under +1 dB overall), a little more tape hiss at -36 dBFS, and the
mallet's opened top. Each is far under +3 dB together with the voice routes already there.

### Tape Flute (01-bedroom.csbank)
fx = src=macro3 dst=f1.size amount=0.15
- Swell: the slower breath also opens into a longer plate (size 0.55 to 0.7, tail about 4.9 s to 7.4 s)

### Vinyl Choir (01-bedroom.csbank)
fx = src=macro3 dst=f3.size amount=0.15
- Bloom: the choir blooms into a bigger room as it swells (size 0.6 to 0.75, tail about 5.5 s to 8.7 s)

### Cassette Keys (01-bedroom.csbank)
fx = src=macro2 dst=f1.hiss amount=0.2
fx = src=macro3 dst=f3.size amount=0.1
- Grit: the tape is more worn as the drive rises (hiss 0.3 to 0.5, about -40 to -36 dBFS under the notes)
- Decay: the longer notes ring into a slightly bigger room (size 0.35 to 0.45, tail about 3.2 s to 3.9 s; kept small so the keys stay close)

### Blanket Pad (01-bedroom.csbank)
fx = src=macro3 dst=f2.size amount=0.15
- Swell: the slow pad swells into a longer plate (size 0.65 to 0.8, tail about 6.3 s to 10.5 s)

### Bedroom Bass (01-bedroom.csbank)
fx = src=macro4 dst=f1.wow amount=0.25
- Wobble: the tape wobbles more with the filter (wow 0.3 to 0.55, slow drift about 4 to 8 cents)

### Dusty Mallet (01-bedroom.csbank)
fx = src=macro1 dst=f1.bandwidth amount=0.2
fx = src=macro3 dst=f2.size amount=0.15
- Hardness: the record's narrow top opens so the harder strike's glint gets through (bandwidth 0.6 to 0.8, about 7 kHz to 12 kHz)
- Ring: the longer ring carries into a bigger room (size 0.35 to 0.5, tail about 3.2 s to 4.4 s)

### Neon Brass (03-outrun.csbank)
fx = src=macro3 dst=f2.feedback amount=0.25
fx = src=macro4 dst=f1.depth amount=0.25
- Swell: the dotted-eighth echo repeats longer as the brass swells (feedback 0.3 to about 0.51)
- Shimmer: the chorus sweeps deeper under the vibrato (depth 0.5 to 0.75, 1.1 ms to 1.65 ms)

### Gated Pad (03-outrun.csbank)
fx = src=macro2 dst=f1.depth amount=0.2
fx = src=macro3 dst=f2.size amount=0.1
- Lush: the chorus sweeps deeper with the wider unison (depth 0.45 to 0.65)
- Fade: the slower pad leaves a longer hall behind the gate (size 0.75 to 0.85, tail about 8.7 s to 13 s)

### Laser Lead (03-outrun.csbank)
fx = src=macro3 dst=f2.feedback amount=0.2
- Sweep: the slower sweep trails more ping-pong echoes (feedback 0.35 to about 0.52)

### Grid Keys (03-outrun.csbank)
fx = src=macro3 dst=f2.feedback amount=0.2
- Decay: the longer notes leave more dotted echoes (feedback 0.25 to about 0.42)

Left without effect routes:
- 00-workshop.csbank, all seven (Basic Saw, Basic Pulse, Basic Sine, Basic Arp, Basic Noise, Basic FM,
  Basic Wavetable): they set no effect slot, so nothing can be routed.
- 02-arcade.csbank, all six (Coin Pulse, Boss Bass, Power-Up, Static Hit, SCC Lead, Chip Chord): four
  have no effect; Coin Pulse and SCC Lead have one slap of delay with feedback 0, and the bank's room
  rule is "at most one slap of delay", so feedback (more repeats) or a longer, unsynced time (a pitch
  bend while turning, and no longer a slap) would break it.
- 03-outrun.csbank: Night Drive (more feedback muddies a sixteenth bass line; more drive on F1 would
  add level and is not what Squelch, the resonance, does) and Sunset Strings (the hall is already at
  size 0.8, about 10.5 s; a deeper phaser would not be what Vibrato says).
