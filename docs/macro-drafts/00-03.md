# Macro drafts: banks 00 to 03 (Workshop, Bedroom, Arcade, Outrun)

Scaling used below (from `ModMatrix.h`): slot parameters move in their normalised range (Ladder
cutoff: 0.1 is about one octave); `pitch` 1 = 48 semitones (0.006 is about 0.29 semitone);
`envN.time` and `lfoN.rate` move by amount x 4 octaves (0.25 = twice as long or fast). No preset in
these four banks has a part B. Every line goes after the preset's existing lines.

## 00-workshop.csbank

### Basic Saw (00-workshop.csbank)
names: Warmth, Grit, Tail, Vibrato
mod = src=macro1 dst=p1.cutoff amount=-0.45
mod = src=macro2 dst=p1.drive amount=0.6
mod = src=macro3 dst=env1.time amount=0.75
mod = src=lfo1 dst=pitch amount=0.006 via=macro4
mod = src=macro4 dst=lfo1.rate amount=0.33
- Warmth: the open filter closes from 20 kHz to about 900 Hz, the saw turns round and warm.
- Grit: up to 14 dB of drive into the ladder, a light saturation (about +2 dB at most).
- Tail: the whole envelope stretches up to 8 times: a 1.2 s release, a softer 16 ms attack.
- Vibrato: a vibrato grows to about 0.3 semitone while its rate climbs from 2 to 5 Hz.

### Grid Keys (03-outrun.csbank)
