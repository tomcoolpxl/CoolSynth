# Effect routes for the macros (D179): banks 08, 09, 10, 12

Normalised amounts converted from `insertDescriptor` (src/synth/v3/Inserts.h): delay feedback is
0..0.85 linear (normalised = units / 0.85, so +0.2 is +0.17 of feedback); reverb size, damping and
mix, chorus depth, pump depth and drive amount and mix are 0..1 linear; EQ high is -12..+12 dB linear
(+0.08 is +1.9 dB). Every line is `src=macroN`, so nothing moves at macro 0. No macro name changes.
Delay feedback at full stays at or under 0.52 (the ceiling of the type is 0.85 in any case).

### Reese (08-main-stage.csbank)
fx = src=macro1 dst=f1.amount amount=0.08
- Bite: also leans on the master drive (amount 0.18 to 0.26), so the opened filter growls instead of only brightening

### Supersaw (08-main-stage.csbank)
fx = src=macro1 dst=f2.damping amount=0.2
fx = src=macro3 dst=f2.size amount=0.1
fx = src=macro3 dst=f1.feedback amount=0.2
- Warmth: also darkens the plate's tail (damping 0.45 to 0.65), so the reverb does not stay bright over a darkened stack
- Tail: also lengthens the plate (size 0.75 to 0.85) and the echoes (feedback 0.25 to about 0.42)
- Part B gets no b.fx lines: it is kept out of the effects (`b.mix fx=0`), has no inserts, and the
  master slots are shared, so part A's routes already move them; a copy on B's macros would double
  the move when both parts' Tail are up.

### Pluck Drop (08-main-stage.csbank)
fx = src=macro3 dst=f1.feedback amount=0.2
fx = src=macro3 dst=f2.size amount=0.1
- Length: also lets the eighth echoes ring on (feedback 0.3 to about 0.47) and widens the plate (size 0.6 to 0.7)

### Hyper Lead (08-main-stage.csbank)
fx = src=macro3 dst=f1.feedback amount=0.2
- Release: also stretches the ping-pong echo trail (feedback 0.35 to about 0.52)

### Future Chord (08-main-stage.csbank)
fx = src=macro1 dst=f3.high amount=0.08
fx = src=macro2 dst=f1.depth amount=0.2
fx = src=macro3 dst=f2.size amount=0.1
fx = src=macro4 dst=f4.depth amount=0.15
- Bright: also lifts the master EQ's air (high shelf +2.5 to about +4.4 dB)
- Detune: also deepens the chorus (depth 0.45 to 0.65), a wider, more seasick stack
- Tail: also widens the plate (size 0.6 to 0.7)
- Wobble: also digs the sidechain pump deeper (depth 0.6 to 0.75), so the chord breathes harder on every quarter as the filter wobbles

### 808 Boom (09-engine-room.csbank)
fx = src=macro1 dst=f1.mix amount=0.2
- Edge: also brings in more of the drive (mix 0.5 to 0.7), the clipped 808 that cuts through small speakers

### Wobble (09-engine-room.csbank)
fx = src=macro2 dst=f1.amount amount=0.1
- Grit: also pushes the master drive (amount 0.25 to 0.35) on top of the ladder's own drive and resonance

### Euclid Pulse (10-clock-tower.csbank)
fx = src=macro3 dst=f1.feedback amount=0.2
- Length: also lets the dotted-eighth echoes fill the rhythm's gaps (feedback 0.3 to about 0.47)

### Ratchet Run (10-clock-tower.csbank)
fx = src=macro3 dst=f1.feedback amount=0.2
- Length: also stretches the ping-pong echoes (feedback 0.25 to about 0.42)

### Step Filter (10-clock-tower.csbank)
fx = src=macro3 dst=f2.size amount=0.15
- Release: also lengthens the plate (size 0.45 to 0.6), so a released chord fades into a longer room

### Random Walk (10-clock-tower.csbank)
fx = src=macro3 dst=f1.feedback amount=0.2
- Length: also stretches the ping-pong echoes (feedback 0.35 to about 0.52)

### Polyrhythm Pad (10-clock-tower.csbank)
fx = src=macro3 dst=f2.size amount=0.1
fx = src=macro4 dst=f1.depth amount=0.25
- Swell: also widens the plate (size 0.7 to 0.8)
- Sway: also deepens the chorus (depth 0.4 to 0.65) along with the pulse-width and cutoff sway

### Chord Trigger (10-clock-tower.csbank)
fx = src=macro3 dst=f1.feedback amount=0.2
- Length: also stretches the bouncing dotted-eighth echoes (feedback 0.35 to about 0.52)

### Contact (12-museum.csbank)
fx = src=macro2 dst=i1.amount amount=0.04
- Grit: also drives the part's insert a little harder (amount 0.05 to 0.09): the desk or tape pushed further, the notes of a chord roughening against each other more. Kept small: it is the record's own roughness, and the drive's small-signal gain rises with it (at most about +2 dB on a quiet signal).

Left without effect routes: Riser (its plate is already a 0.85 space and the preset is the loudest
at full, +3.7 dB), Neuro Growl (no effects), Pure Sub, FM Knock, Donk, Fat Analog (no effects: the
Engine Room is tight and dry), and every Museum preset but Contact: DX7 E.PIANO 1,
DX7 BASS 1, Prophet-5 Sync II, Prophet-5 Brass, Minimoog Fat Bass, TX81Z LatelyBass, Contact
Opening I, Contact Opening II, Minimoog Trilogy Lead (no effect slots: the sounds are as the
instrument made them, and an empty slot cannot be routed).
