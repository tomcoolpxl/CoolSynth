# Macro drafts: Main Stage, Engine Room, Clock Tower, Museum

Every line below is new and adds nothing with its macro at 0. Amounts follow ModMatrix.h: slot
parameters move in their normalised range (cutoff 0.1 is about one octave), envelope times and LFO
rates move by amount x 4 octaves (0.25 doubles, 0.5 is four times), pitch 1 = 48 semitones (0.006 is
about 0.29 semitone). A vibrato or wobble on an LFO the preset does not use starts from that LFO's
default (sine, 2 Hz, restarts with each note), so its Movement macro also raises the rate (0.36 takes
2 Hz to about 5.5 Hz at full; 0.25 to 4 Hz; 0.3 to 4.6 Hz). Presets whose LFO already does something
keep its rate.

## 12 Museum

envelope rates and the LFO's pitch or amplitude depth; the Prophet-5 through cutoff, a harder-driven
filter input, Osc A frequency, the filter envelope and the wheel-mod vibrato; the Minimoog through
cutoff, emphasis, the filter-modulation amount and the contours.
