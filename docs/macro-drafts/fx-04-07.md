# Effect routes for the macros (D179): banks 04-07

Drafted from docs/macro-drafts/BRIEF-fx.md. Every line below is new and comes from `src=macroN`, so
with every macro at 0 nothing changes. No route raises a wet mix: the only level that can rise is
a reverb's tail, from a longer size or more shimmer. No macro name changes.

Values used (from `insertDescriptor` in src/synth/v3/Inserts.h and src/synth/PlateReverb.h):
- Reverb size is linear 0..1. The plate's decay is 0.25 + 0.70 x size, so RT60 is about
  2.26 s / -ln(decay) (this matches the measured 2.41 / 4.36 / 10.49 s at 0.2 / 0.5 / 0.8, D178).
  That gives 0.4: 3.5 s, 0.6: 5.6 s, 0.7: 7.4 s, 0.8: 10.7 s, 0.85: 13 s, 0.9: 18 s, 0.95: 25 s.
  Size 1 is decay 0.95, which is long but finite.
- Delay feedback is linear 0..0.85, so +0.1 normalised adds 0.085. It can never go past 0.85.
- Chorus rate is logarithmic 0.05..10 Hz, so +0.08 normalised is a factor of about 1.53.
- Chorus depth, phaser depth, reverb shimmer and modulation, and LoFi wow and crackle are all
  linear 0..1.
- Master slots in a layer preset (Deep Field, Juno Pad): both parts route the same master slot, as
  the brief's "same four macros for part B" asks. With both parts' knobs full the two amounts add.
  I set the amounts so that the two together stay musical, and each section says where they end up.

## 04-observatory.csbank

### Solar Wind (04-observatory.csbank)
fx = src=macro3 dst=f1.size amount=0.05
- Swell: the plate also grows from size 0.9 to 0.95 (a tail of about 18 s to about 25 s), so the
  slower release falls into a longer space.

### Nebula Pad (04-observatory.csbank)
fx = src=macro2 dst=f1.depth amount=0.25
fx = src=macro3 dst=f2.size amount=0.05
- Thickness: the chorus also deepens (depth 0.35 to 0.6) along with the wavetables' detune.
- Swell: the plate also grows from 0.9 to 0.95 (about 18 s to 25 s).

### Deep Field (04-observatory.csbank)
fx = src=macro3 dst=f1.size amount=0.05
b.fx = src=macro3 dst=f1.size amount=0.05
- Swell: the shared plate also grows from 0.85 to 0.9 (about 13 s to 18 s) from either part's
  Swell. With both parts' Swell full it reaches 0.95 (about 25 s).

### Shimmer Drone (04-observatory.csbank)
fx = src=macro1 dst=f1.shimmer amount=0.15
fx = src=macro3 dst=f1.size amount=0.05
fx = src=macro4 dst=f1.modulation amount=0.3
- Glow: the octave haze above the note also gets stronger (shimmer 0.6 to 0.75) as the filter opens.
- Swell: the plate also grows from 0.85 to 0.9 (about 13 s to 18 s).
- Waver: the plate's own tank modulation also deepens (0.5, the default, to 0.8), so the tail
  wavers along with the filter wobble.
- The shimmer cannot run away: each pass goes up an octave inside a 180 Hz to 5 kHz band, so it
  leaves the band after a few passes, and the tank's decay stays at or below 0.88.

### Satellite Ping (04-observatory.csbank)
fx = src=macro3 dst=f1.feedback amount=0.1
- Ring: the ping-pong echo also repeats longer (feedback 0.7 to about 0.79, under the 0.85 limit).
  The sum of the echoes rises by about 1.2 dB on the wet side.

### Granular Sky (04-observatory.csbank)
fx = src=macro3 dst=f1.size amount=0.05
- Swell: the plate also grows from 0.9 to 0.95 (about 18 s to 25 s).

### Live Shimmer (04-observatory.csbank)
fx = src=macro2 dst=f1.depth amount=0.2
fx = src=macro3 dst=f2.size amount=0.05
- Shimmer: the chorus also deepens (depth 0.4 to 0.6) along with the saw's detune and the grain spray.
- Swell: the plate also grows from 0.85 to 0.9 (about 13 s to 18 s).

### Frozen Pluck (04-observatory.csbank)
fx = src=macro3 dst=f1.size amount=0.08
- Tail: the plate also grows from 0.8 to 0.88 (about 11 s to 16 s), so the longer string and
  cloud ring out into a longer space.

## 06-basement.csbank

### Furnace Drone (06-basement.csbank)

### Concrete Pad (06-basement.csbank)
fx = src=macro2 dst=f2.crackle amount=0.15
fx = src=macro3 dst=f3.size amount=0.1
fx = src=macro4 dst=f2.wow amount=0.3
- Crush: the dirty tape also crackles more (crackle 0.15 to 0.3) as the bits drop. The crackle
  follows the signal's level.
- Swell: the reverb also grows from 0.6 to 0.7 (about 5.6 s to 7.4 s).
- Sway: the tape's wow also deepens (0.25 to 0.55, a slow 0.45 Hz drift of about 3 to about 7
  cents), so the pitch sways with the filter.

### Siren (06-basement.csbank)
fx = src=macro3 dst=f2.feedback amount=0.05
- Fade: the long echo also holds longer (feedback 0.8 to about 0.84, under the 0.85 limit; the drive
  is before the delay, not in its loop). The sum of the echoes rises by about 1.1 dB on the wet side.

## 07-studio-1982.csbank

### Juno Pad (07-studio-1982.csbank)
fx = src=macro3 dst=f2.size amount=0.1
fx = src=macro4 dst=f1.rate amount=0.08
fx = src=macro4 dst=f1.depth amount=0.15
b.fx = src=macro3 dst=f2.size amount=0.1
b.fx = src=macro4 dst=f1.rate amount=0.08
b.fx = src=macro4 dst=f1.depth amount=0.15
- Swell: the shared reverb also grows from 0.55 to 0.65 (about 5 s to 6.6 s) from either part.
  With both parts full it reaches 0.75.
- Swirl: the shared chorus also speeds up and deepens, from 0.5 Hz to about 0.76 Hz and depth
  0.55 to 0.7. That goes from the slow Chorus I toward the faster Chorus II (the Juno-60's two
  buttons are about 0.4 and 0.6 Hz, per Wikipedia). With both parts' Swirl full it reaches about
  1.2 Hz and depth 0.85.

### OB Strings (07-studio-1982.csbank)
fx = src=macro2 dst=f1.depth amount=0.25
fx = src=macro3 dst=f2.size amount=0.1
- Ensemble: the phaser also deepens (depth 0.6 to 0.85) as the two saws spread, the classic
  string-machine-through-a-phaser thickening.
- Swell: the reverb also grows from 0.65 to 0.75 (about 6.5 s to 8.9 s).

### Sync Lead (07-studio-1982.csbank)
fx = src=macro3 dst=f1.feedback amount=0.2
- Sweep: the echo also repeats longer (feedback 0.3 to about 0.47) as the sweep slows. The sum of
  the echoes rises by about 0.7 dB on the wet side, at a mix of 0.18.

## Left without effect routes

Harp String, Acid, Rust Bass, Metal Hit, Prophet Brass, Moog Bass, Clav Keys.
- Harp String: its Room is set to match the recording. A bigger room would undo that.
- Acid, Rust Bass, Clav Keys: their Character macros already push a drive. Raising the master
  drive too adds level on a hot signal: drive gain is about 1 + 15 x amount before tanh.
- Metal Hit: it was already over +4 dB in D177 and was turned down, so it has no headroom left for
  a longer reverb.
- Prophet Brass: a dry played patch with a small room. More space did not help any of its macros.
- Moog Bass: it has no effects.
