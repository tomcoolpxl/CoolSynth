Reading the current

Dots that travel are a pulse or a charge. Dots that shake are sound: current going back and forth. Thicker means more current. A wire's tint is its voltage:above ground,below. The six oscillators and the noise never stop; a strike only lets them through. The currents here are drawn by hand for the mockup; the plugin would draw the solver's own.
