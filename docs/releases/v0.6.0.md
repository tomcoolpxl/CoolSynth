The fourth release of CoolSynth, a bi-timbral, multi-engine hybrid synthesizer, and the first for the Mac. It runs as a standalone app, or as a VST3 or CLAP plugin in your DAW, on Windows and on Macs with Apple silicon.

## Install

On Windows: download `CoolSynth-Setup-v0.6.0.exe` below and run it. It updates 0.3.0 to 0.5.0 in place; your own sounds and samples stay.

Windows may warn that the publisher is unknown, because the installer isn't code-signed. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

On a Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 13 or newer: open `CoolSynth-Setup-v0.6.0.pkg`. It isn't signed, so macOS blocks it once: go to **System Settings > Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**. It installs for your user only, without an administrator password.

## What's new

- On the Mac
  - CoolSynth runs on Macs with Apple silicon: the app, the VST3 and the CLAP, with all 139 presets and the recorded instruments.
  - This first Mac version is not yet tuned for speed, and hasn't been tried with the Arturia MiniLab 3 or an Audio Unit host such as Logic or GarageBand.
- Sound
  - Three new sound engines play the Roland TR-808's drums from their circuits: 808 Drums (bass drum, toms, congas, rim shot, claves), 808 Snare & Clap (snare, hand clap, maracas) and 808 Metal (cowbell, cymbal, hi-hats).
  - A new Kit mode plays them as a drum kit, one drum per key. New preset in Studio 1982: 808 Kit.
  - At 88.2 kHz and higher, the voices run at the host's rate instead of twice it, so CoolSynth costs about half as much there and sounds the same.
  - Brightness no longer clicks when you sweep it all the way while a chord rings.
  - When a kit is too heavy for your computer, it drops its quietest drum instead of going silent.

Projects and sounds saved with 0.3.0 to 0.5.0 open as they were.

## Files

- `CoolSynth-Setup-v0.6.0.exe` is the Windows installer. Start here on Windows.
- `CoolSynth-Setup-v0.6.0.pkg` is the Mac installer. Start here on a Mac.
- `CoolSynth-windows-x64-vst3-v0.6.0.zip`, `-clap-` and `-standalone-` hold just the VST3, the CLAP or the app for Windows.
- `CoolSynth-macos-arm64-vst3-v0.6.0.zip`, `-clap-` and `-standalone-` hold the same for the Mac. macOS blocks these unsigned downloads, so use the installer if you can.
- `CoolSynth-windows-x64-sha256-v0.6.0.txt` and `CoolSynth-macos-arm64-sha256-v0.6.0.txt` have checksums for the other files.
