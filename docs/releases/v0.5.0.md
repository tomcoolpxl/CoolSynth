The third release of CoolSynth, a bi-timbral, multi-engine hybrid synthesizer for Windows. It runs as a standalone app, or as a VST3 or CLAP plugin in your DAW.

## Install

Download `CoolSynth-Setup-v0.5.0.exe` below and run it. It updates 0.3.0 and 0.4.0 in place; your own sounds and samples stay.

Windows may warn that the publisher is unknown, because the installer isn't code-signed. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

The installer puts the VST3 in `C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3`, the CLAP in `C:\Program Files\Common Files\CLAP` and the app in the Start menu. The sampled instruments you already have stay; untick them to skip the download.

## What's new

Three new sound engines: classic sound chips, a kick drum and the TB-303. There are 138 presets.

- Sound
  - Chip: the NES, Game Boy, C64 SID, AY, YM and Konami's SCC, with their frame-rate arpeggios and drums. The Arcade room is rebuilt on it.
  - Kick: a tuned kick drum, with the TR-808's and the TR-909's bass drum circuits and a modern kick. New preset: Succession 808.
  - TB-303: the bass line's saw to square through its diode ladder, with its accent and slide. Acid plays on it.
  - The state-variable filter morphs from low-pass to high-pass as the Oberheim SEM does, and has the C64 SID's two filter characters.
  - New preset: Ladder Chords. Tape Flute, High Voice and Four-Track House are gone.
  - A sampled instrument no longer clicks when you strike a key again while it still rings.
- In your DAW
  - The macros carry the playing sound's names, such as "A1 Bite", and follow it when you change sound.
  - The screen shows the tempo the sound plays at, also while your DAW is stopped.
- Arturia MiniLab 3
  - Ableton Live loads CoolSynth's MiniLab script by itself, and it arms the MIDI track you select.
  - FL Studio picks CoolSynth's MiniLab script by itself.
  - For Bitwig Studio, the installer adds a CoolSynth MiniLab 3 controller script, so the pads switch sections instead of launching clips.

Projects and sounds saved with 0.3.0 and 0.4.0 open as they were.

## Files

- `CoolSynth-Setup-v0.5.0.exe` is the installer. Start here.
- `CoolSynth-windows-x64-vst3-v0.5.0.zip` holds just the VST3.
- `CoolSynth-windows-x64-clap-v0.5.0.zip` holds just the CLAP.
- `CoolSynth-windows-x64-standalone-v0.5.0.zip` holds just the app.
- `CoolSynth-windows-x64-sha256-v0.5.0.txt` has checksums for the other files.
