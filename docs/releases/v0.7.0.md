The fifth release of CoolSynth, a bi-timbral, multi-engine hybrid synthesizer. It runs as a standalone app, or as a VST3 or CLAP plugin in your DAW, on Windows.

## Install

Download `CoolSynth-Setup-v0.7.0.exe` below and run it. It updates 0.3.0 to 0.6.0 in place; your own sounds and samples stay.

Windows may warn that the publisher is unknown, because the installer isn't code-signed. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

This release is for Windows only. The Mac comes back in a later release; until then, 0.6.0 is the version for Macs.

## What's new

- Sound
  - The TR-808 circuits (the 808 Kit and the Kick's 808) are solved faster, with the same sound.
  - The Kick engine offers the 808 and Modern models. The 909 model has left the menu; a sound saved with it plays as the 808.
- Siblings
  - CoolSynth 303 and CoolSynth 808 are released as their own apps, on the same sound engine: see their pages for [CoolSynth 303](https://github.com/tomcoolpxl/CoolSynth303) and [CoolSynth 808](https://github.com/tomcoolpxl/CoolSynth808).
- The notices file now lists every library CoolSynth includes, with its licence.

Projects and sounds saved with 0.3.0 to 0.6.0 open as they were.

## Files

- `CoolSynth-Setup-v0.7.0.exe` is the installer. Start here.
- `CoolSynth-windows-x64-vst3-v0.7.0.zip`, `-clap-` and `-standalone-` hold just the VST3, the CLAP or the app.
- `CoolSynth-windows-x64-sha256-v0.7.0.txt` has checksums for the other files.
