The first release of CoolSynth: a two-layer synthesizer for Windows, as a VST3 plugin for your music software
(DAW) and as a standalone app.

## Install

Download **CoolSynth-Setup-v0.3.0.exe** below and run it. It installs:

- the **VST3** where every DAW looks (`C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3`): let your DAW rescan its plugins
  and CoolSynth is listed as an instrument;
- the **app**, with a Start menu entry;
- the **recorded instruments** you tick (2.6 GB for all seven families), downloaded from the
  [CoolSynth instrument library](https://huggingface.co/datasets/tomccargo/coolsynth-instruments) and checked
  before they are unpacked. Without them everything else plays; those sounds stay silent.

Windows may warn that the installer comes from an unknown publisher, because it is not code-signed: choose
**More info**, then **Run anyway**.

Requires 64-bit Windows 10 or 11 (made and tested on Windows 11).

## What is in it

- **Two layers**, each a whole sound, played stacked, split across the keyboard, or by velocity.
  Right-click a layer on the screen to copy, swap or double it.
- **Nine sound sources**, three per layer at once: virtual analogue, noise, two- and four-operator FM,
  wavetables, a plucked string, struck bars and bells, recorded instruments, and grains.
- **Seven filters and shapers**, three per voice: a ladder and a state-variable filter, a bit crusher, a
  wavefolder, drive, a vowel filter and a comb.
- **Movement**: envelopes, LFOs, a 16-step modulator, a modulation matrix, four macros per sound, chord memory
  and an arpeggiator with Euclidean rhythms, ratchets and chance.
- **Thirteen effects** in six slots per layer and six at the end: drive, lo-fi, phaser, flanger, chorus, tape
  and ping-pong delay, plate reverb with shimmer, room, compressor, EQ, pump, grains, and a pitch shifter.
- **136 sounds in 14 rooms**, from synthwave and techno to the Night Bus room, the Museum's rebuilt classic
  synths and the Conservatory's 40 recorded acoustic instruments. A new CoolSynth starts on **Reese** from the
  Main Stage room.
- **Your own sounds**: a WAV file played across the keys, cut into slices, or a folder of files played as a kit.
- **Stop** (the white square pad) ends every sound at once, echoes and reverb included.
- **Prefs** shows which version you have.

## Your keyboard or controller

Any MIDI keyboard plays CoolSynth, and its knobs, faders, pads and buttons can work CoolSynth's panel:

- **Known controllers** set themselves up when plugged in: Akai MPK mini mk3, Novation Launchkey Mini MK3 and
  MK4, Arturia KeyLab Essential mk3, NI Komplete Kontrol M32 and A series.
- **Any other**: **Prefs > Controller setup... > Set up step by step** asks for each knob, fader and pad in turn.
- **One at a time**: right-click a knob, fader or pad on the panel and choose **Learn**.

**FL Studio** passes a controller's knobs to a plugin only on a shared MIDI port: in **Options > MIDI
settings** give your keyboard a **Port** number, then in CoolSynth's window open the plugin wrapper's
**Settings** tab and set **MIDI > Input port** to the same number.

## Arturia MiniLab 3

CoolSynth uses the MiniLab 3's screen, pads and knobs, in the app and in your DAW: the screen shows the sound and
the knob you turn, the pads open the sections. Using it inside a DAW needs Windows MIDI Services (Windows 11
24H2 or later, through Windows Update).

So that the pads do not also play notes in your DAW, the installer adds a small script for **FL Studio** (it
links itself to the MiniLab) and **Ableton Live**: there, choose **CoolSynth_MiniLab** once as a Control Surface
(**Settings > Link, Tempo & MIDI**) with Input **Minilab3 MIDI** and Output **None**. To use the MiniLab as a plain
controller instead, set **Prefs > Controller setup... > MiniLab 3 driver** to **Off**.

## The files below

- **CoolSynth-Setup-v0.3.0.exe**: the installer. Start here.
- **CoolSynth-windows-x64-vst3-v0.3.0.zip**: the VST3 alone (copy `CoolSynth.vst3` into
  `C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3`).
- **CoolSynth-windows-x64-standalone-v0.3.0.zip**: the app alone.
- **CoolSynth-windows-x64-sha256-v0.3.0.txt**: checksums, to check a download.

Without the installer, the instrument families are zips on the
[instrument library's page](https://huggingface.co/datasets/tomccargo/coolsynth-instruments); their folders go in
`C:\ProgramData\CoolSynth\Library`.

The full guide: [CoolSynth on GitHub](https://github.com/tomcoolpxl/CoolSynth#readme).
